# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Continuity Studio welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome different filmmaking traditions, languages, and technical skill levels.
- Respect privacy, copyright, consent, and the dignity of people represented in reference media.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publication of private material are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns through the repository’s private maintainer contact or GitHub reporting tools. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
