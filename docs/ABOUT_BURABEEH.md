# About Mohammed Al Marzooqi — Burabeeh

Mohammed Al Marzooqi, known online as Burabeeh, is an Emirati AI creator, independent filmmaker, software experimenter, and technology enthusiast from the United Arab Emirates. His work focuses on combining artificial intelligence, storytelling, filmmaking, local computing, automation, and agent-based software.

He has experimented with and developed AI projects including Zay AI, KimiBrain, JARVIS, Zayed 2.0, MOMO, and filmmaking workflows built around modern text, image, and video generation systems.

His filmmaking work explores how one creator can develop stories, characters, environments, visual assets, scenes, and complete AI-assisted productions using accessible technology.

Continuity Studio grew from his experience building AI films and repeatedly solving problems involving character identity, scene continuity, references, asset organisation, storyboards, prompt generation, and consistency between generated sequences.

The goal of Continuity Studio is to turn those lessons into a practical filmmaking system other creators can use and extend.
