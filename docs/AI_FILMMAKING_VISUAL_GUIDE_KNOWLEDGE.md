# AI Filmmaking Visual Guide — integrated knowledge source

Continuity Studio integrates the user-supplied `AI_Filmmaking_Visual_Guide.pdf` as a summarized, inspectable knowledge source. The source record is identified by SHA-256 `fc621f014cf54a2796a8b185b3ecaaa15c42cc79e46e6c1d0ec66ff76b9ee958` and is represented operationally in `server/filmmaking-knowledge.ts` and `rules/visual_guide_rules.json`.

The durable principles are:

1. Stable identity anchors are copied unchanged between character-sheet, storyboard, and video prompts.
2. Character identity sheets use neutral, even lighting and simple backgrounds; transient scene state stays outside the identity master.
3. A compact style anchor carries palette, light, lens, texture or grain, camera language, and aspect ratio.
4. Prompts use concrete, high-signal production direction, contain no unresolved placeholders, and avoid repeated prose.
5. The optional nine-panel Storyboard Grid is a continuous ordered scene derived from the Shot Planner. It never replaces the Shot Planner.
6. Every distinct reference receives a unique temporary platform upload position while permanent Project Image numbers and filenames remain unchanged.
7. Character sheets control identity; the Storyboard Grid controls motion, framing, and geography; prompt text controls action, timing, performance, dialogue, sound, and ending state.
8. Provider limits and syntax belong to versioned Platform Profiles.

The guide's Seedance 2.0 duration and audio claims are source-era observations, not global defaults. Continuity Studio therefore does not hard-code them into project law.
